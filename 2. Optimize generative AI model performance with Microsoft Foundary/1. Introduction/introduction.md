# Optimizing Generative AI Models — Introduction

## 1. Why Optimize a Model?

A **base language model** is powerful, but it may not fully meet the requirements of a specific application.

The quality, accuracy, and consistency of its responses depend on **how the model is configured and augmented**.

### Example: Travel Agency ✈️

A travel company wants to build a chatbot.

A base model can answer general travel questions, but the company needs it to:

-   🗣️ Follow the company's **tone of voice**
-   🏨 Provide accurate information from its **hotel catalog**
-   📋 Maintain a **consistent response format**

So, the base model needs to be **optimized** for the specific requirements.

---

## 2. Optimization Strategies

There are several complementary ways to improve a generative AI model.

They range from:

**Quick + Low Cost**  
→ Simple configuration and prompt-based approaches

**More Involved**  
→ Techniques requiring additional **time, resources, and effort**

These strategies can be used:

-   **Individually**
-   **In combination**

---

### 🧠 Key Idea

> **Base Model + Optimization Strategies → Better Performance for a Specific Application**

### 🔥 Remember

**Quality + Accuracy + Consistency = Model Configuration & Augmentation**

The rest of the module focuses on **which optimization strategies are available, when to use them, and how to apply them.**
