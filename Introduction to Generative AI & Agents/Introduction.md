# Generative AI: Introduction

## What is Generative AI?

Generative AI = AI that can create original content, such as:

✍️ Text
🎨 Images
💻 Computer code
🎵 Other content

It may seem like “magic”, but it is actually based on mathematics, statistics, data science, and machine learning.

## Main Idea

Generative AI isn't magic → it's the result of years of research and mathematical techniques.

Understanding its core concepts and principles helps you understand how modern generative AI and AI agents work.

## Remember

Generative AI → Creates content → Powered by Math + Statistics + Data Science + ML
